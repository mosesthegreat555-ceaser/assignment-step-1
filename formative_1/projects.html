Our Programmes & Projects

Practical support for learners preparing for life after school.

University & College Guidance

We explain what different institutions offer and help learners choose the right options for their interests.

  • Information about applications and requirements
  • Simple comparisons to help choose a path
  • Practical tips for getting started

Bursary & Scholarship Support

We point learners to funding opportunities and explain the application basics so they can apply with confidence.

  • Lists of common bursaries and deadlines
  • Help with application documents
  • Advice on budgeting and affordability

Career Readiness

Short workshops and activities to help learners explore careers and set practical goals.

  • Career exploration sessions
  • Goal setting and next-step planning
  • Basic life skills for study and work

School Partnerships

We work with schools to bring information and sessions directly to learners who need them.

  • Workshops and school visits
  • Community outreach
  • Cooperation with local education partners

Why these programmes matter

Our programmes are built to turn uncertainty into clear, practical steps so learners can move forward with confidence.

  • Greater awareness of opportunities
  • Better access to support and information
  • More confidence in choosing the right next step

Need support?

If you are a learner, parent, school, or partner looking for guidance, we are ready to help.
